## About This Policy

FlowZa AI (operated by **SoarTek LLC**, Muscat, Oman) uses cookies and similar technologies
to keep you signed in, understand how our products are used, and personalize your
experience. This policy lists the cookies used across flowza.ai and the FlowZa platforms.

## Essential Cookies

Required for the services to function — these cannot be switched off.

| Cookie              | Purpose                                           | Duration |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| `flowza_session`    | Keeps you signed in during a session              | Session  |
| `flowza_csrf`       | Protects forms against cross-site request forgery | Session  |
| `flowza_auth_token` | Remembers your sign-in between visits             | 30 days  |
| `cookie_consent`    | Stores your cookie preferences                    | 1 year   |

## Analytics Cookies

Help us understand product usage so we can improve it. Set only inside the FlowZa platforms,
not on the marketing site.

| Cookie       | Purpose                              | Duration |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------ | -------- |
| `_ga`        | Google Analytics — usage measurement | 2 years  |
| `posthog_id` | Product analytics — feature usage    | 1 year   |

## Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences inside the platforms.

| Cookie           | Purpose                   | Duration |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- | -------- |
| `flowza_lang`    | Preferred language        | 1 year   |
| `flowza_theme`   | Light/dark theme choice   | 1 year   |
| `flowza_sidebar` | Sidebar layout preference | 30 days  |

## Marketing Cookies

Used only where you have consented, to measure campaign performance.

| Cookie      | Purpose                  | Duration |
| ----------- | ------------------------ | -------- |
| `_fbp`      | Meta ads measurement     | 3 months |
| `li_fat_id` | LinkedIn ads measurement | 30 days  |

## Managing Cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; essential cookies are
required for sign-in to work. Where consent applies, you can change your choice via the
cookie preferences link in the product.
